About the Program

Strategic Foresight, Unshakable Focus, Masterful Play

National Smart Chess is a small-cohort academy that turns curious beginners into confident, composed thinkers. Over eight focused weeks, young players learn how to plan ahead, weigh risk against reward and own every decision they make, one move at a time.

Strategy, one move at a time
Why It Matters

What Chess Builds Beyond the Board

Spatial Reasoning & Math Aptitude

Players calculate sequential branching paths and visualize board states several moves ahead.

Sustained Attention Span

Deep, screen-free immersion that trains patience and quiet, focused discipline.

Calculated Decision-Making

Young thinkers learn to assess risk versus reward, manage the clock and take responsibility for every choice.

Emotional Composure

Resilience through gracious victory, objective loss analysis and constructive post-game review.

The Curriculum

8-Week Curriculum Blueprint

A structured path from the first move to the first tournament.

  1. 01
    Week 1

    The Board & First Checkmates

    Board geography, piece values, algebraic notation and basic checkmate concepts, including defending against Fool’s Mate and Scholar’s Mate.

  2. 02
    Week 2

    Opening Fundamentals

    The golden rules: control the center, develop minor pieces, castle early and avoid unnecessary pawn moves.

  3. 03
    Week 3

    Tactical Weapons I

    Forks, pins and skewers. Recognizing vulnerabilities on opponent squares.

  4. 04
    Week 4

    Tactical Weapons II

    Discovered attacks, double checks, deflections and back-rank vulnerabilities.

  5. 05
    Week 5

    Middle-Game Strategy

    Outposts, open files, piece activity and pawn structure fundamentals.

  6. 06
    Week 6

    Essential Endgames

    King and Queen vs. King, King and Rook vs. King, and the Rule of the Square for pawn promotion.

  7. 07
    Week 7

    Clock Management & Match Protocol

    Touch-move rule, handling time scrambles and psychological composure.

  8. 08
    Week 8

    NAA Rapid Chess Showcase

    Internal round-robin mini-tournament, individual performance analysis and certificates.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What ages and levels is National Smart Chess for?

Players aged 6 to 17, from complete beginners to intermediate players. Coaches adapt the pace and positions to each player in the cohort.

How big are the groups?

Each cohort is strictly capped at 10 students so every player gets real coaching attention and playing time.

How long is the program?

Eight weeks, one 75-minute session per week, finishing with the NAA Rapid Chess Showcase mini-tournament.

When does registration open and what does it cost?

Registration has not opened yet. Dates, venue and pricing will be announced soon. Use the notify button and we will email you the moment seats are released.

Does my child need to know how to play already?

No. Week 1 starts with the board, the pieces and notation, so complete beginners are welcome.
