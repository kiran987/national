Your Child's 8-Session Journey

Every session is a new challenge and a new martial arts style. Schedule and session timings are currently finalizing.

Session 01🥋
Stage 1

Kick Power!

(Karate Kicks)

Build balance, flexibility, coordination, and confidence with dynamic karate kicks.

Session 02🥊
Stage 2

Punch Like a Champ!

(Boxing Basics)

Discover fundamental punches, footwork, speed, and coordination through energetic boxing drills.

Session 03🐉
Stage 3

Warrior Moves!

(Kalaripayattu)

Explore ancient animal postures and movements that build agility, balance, and core strength.

Session 04🥢
Stage 4

Stick & Flow!

(Filipino Arnis & Redonda)

Develop reaction time, rhythm, and double-hand coordination using safe, padded training tools.

Session 05🛡️
Stage 5

Strike & Shield!

(Karate Blocks & Counters)

Master defensive shields and controlled strikes while honing focus and discipline.

Session 06🐅
Stage 6

Agility Warriors!

(Kalaripayattu Flow)

Challenge reflexes with fluid animal forms and dynamic floor movement patterns.

Session 07💪
Stage 7

Brave, Bold & Bully-Proof!

(Safety & Boundary Setting)

Equip kids with assertive body language, verbal boundaries, de-escalation, and situational awareness.

Session 08🏆
Stage 8

Shadow Boxing Showdown!

(Footwork & Flow)

Tie it all together with rhythm, speed combos, and a final celebration of progress!

Safe & Supportive

Strong Body. Confident Mind. Safe Choices.

We don't teach kids to look for fights - we teach them to avoid them, respond confidently, stay safe and get help. Every coach is screened, trained in Safe Sport and First Aid/CPR, and NAA follows the Rule of Two: two screened adults, always, in an open and observable setting.

  • ✓Criminal Record + Vulnerable Sector Check
  • ✓Safe Sport trained coaches
  • ✓First Aid + CPR certified
  • ✓Padded, kid-safe training equipment

Parent FAQs

Is this a fight club or contact program?

No. National Shield is a fitness-first, safety-focused kids' program. Drills use pads, shields and safe padded training tools, and we teach children to avoid conflict, respond confidently, and get help.

Do my child and I need martial arts experience?

Not at all. Every session is built for beginners and introduces a new style from scratch, so kids can join in with confidence.

When will the schedule and timings be confirmed?

We are currently finalizing the upcoming session schedule and timing details. Registered families will receive full confirmation of dates and class hours via email as soon as the final schedule is locked in.

What should my child bring?

Comfortable athletic clothing, clean indoor footwear and a water bottle. Training pads and equipment are provided.

Are the coaches screened?

Yes. Every coach holds a Criminal Record + Vulnerable Sector Check, Safe Sport training, and First Aid/CPR. NAA follows the Rule of Two: two screened adults are always present in an open, observable setting.

Where will classes be held?

Sessions will be held at a premier indoor gymnasium facility in Regina, SK (venue and location TBA). Final facility confirmation and class timings will be announced prior to launch. The full 8-class program is $120 CAD.

How do I register?

The full 8-class program is $120 CAD. Complete the registration form at the top of this page. Our team will confirm placement, venue details, and payment options by email within 2 business days.
