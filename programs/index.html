The NAA Athlete Pathway

A progressive continuum from early toddler movement through multi-sport mastery, competitive showcases, and active lifelong recreation.

1

Active Start

Ages 2–5

Active Movement & Agility (Starting Soon)

Joyful movement exploration, balance, listening skills, and early motor confidence

2

FUNdamentals

Fundamental movement skills across core sports; multi-sport sampling priority

3

Technical skill acquisition, small-sided game IQ, sport sampling maintained

4

6

Ages All Ages

Lifelong fitness, camaraderie, competitive recreation, and community belonging

Structured Developmental Feedback For Every Athlete

The NAA Athlete Progress Report

Most recreational leagues in Regina offer participation without structured developmental feedback. At NAA, every athlete receives an official end-of-block Progress Report created by certified coaches, tracking specific technical, tactical, physical, and character benchmarks.

Technical Competency

Sport-specific motor skills graded on an objective developmental rubric.

Physical Literacy & Athletic Base

Agility, balance, sprint mechanics, and spatial awareness evaluated against age-appropriate LTAD standards.

Tactical & Decision-Making IQ

Court/field vision, defensive positioning, small-sided game decision speed, and strategic understanding.

Character, Effort & Leadership

Coachability, sportsmanship, peer encouragement, resilience after errors, and adherence to Respect in Sport values.
