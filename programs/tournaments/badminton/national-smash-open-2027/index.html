Why National Smash Open 2027?

Official Governance & Terms

Tournament Policies & Disclaimers

Please review all four tournament policies before registering.

1Independent Open Event Status

National Smash Open 2027 is an independent, unsanctioned tournament organized by National Athletics Academy Inc. It is not affiliated with or sanctioned by Badminton Canada or Sask Badminton. No provincial membership is required to participate, and matches will not count toward provincial or national ranking points.

2Format & Schedule Adjustment Rights

Tournament management reserves the right to:

  • Combine under-subscribed age categories or convert brackets into round-robin pool play based on final entry counts.
  • Adjust match scoring formats (e.g., set length and point caps) to guarantee that all events conclude within venue rental hours.
  • Enforce strict 3-minute walkover/default rules to keep courts moving on time.
3Withdrawal & Refund Policy
  • Full refund (minus a $5 administrative processing fee) for cancellations requested in writing prior to the registration deadline (February 15, 2027).
  • No refunds will be issued for withdrawals, injuries, or no-shows after the draws and schedule have been published on February 20, 2027.
4Injury, Liability & Media Notice

Badminton is a high-intensity, physically demanding sport. All athletes participate at their own risk. By registering, players (and parent/legal guardians for minor athletes) acknowledge personal responsibility for injury, property loss, or damage, and consent to the use of tournament photography and video for event promotional purposes.
