Our Story & Governance

About NAA

National Athletics Academy Inc. — an incorporated non-profit multi-sport organization based in Regina, Saskatchewan, dedicated to accessible, structured youth athletic development.

Who We Are & Our Purpose

Why National Athletics Academy Exists

National Athletics Academy Inc. is a registered non-profit corporation in Saskatchewan (Entity No. 102242108), headquartered at 217 Broad St N, Regina, SK S4R 2X7. The organization was established to address a persistent gap in youth athletics: parents in Regina were forced to choose between unstructured, informal drop-in activities or expensive, single-sport travel clubs that push premature specialization.

NAA exists to give Regina families a structured, multi-sport alternative. Under certified coaching staff and unified non-profit governance, we provide foundational skill development, age-appropriate physical literacy, and personal safety instruction across Basketball, Volleyball, Badminton, and National Shield - Martial Arts — all through one transparent, affordable organization.

The NAA Crest

The circular emblem features a maple leaf, multi-sport athlete silhouettes, and stars framed by the National Athletics Academy mark. It represents high-quality coaching, multi-sport accessibility, and Saskatchewan community athletic development.

Mission, Vision & Values

Mission

To make high-quality, structured, multi-sport training accessible to every family in Regina — building physical literacy, competitive ability and character in athletes of all ages, across all sports, from one trusted non-profit academy.

Vision

To be Saskatchewan's leading multi-sport academy: the first place a family looks when a child wants to try a sport, when a youth athlete wants to get better, and when an adult wants to get stronger.

Excellence

Certified coaches, structured curricula, measurable athlete progress. No filler sessions.

Access

Newcomer outreach, equipment lending, and financial assistance support. No child turned away for cost alone.

Safety

Screened coaches, Respect in Sport Certified, age-appropriate loading, concussion protocols.

Multi-Sport

Athletes are encouraged to play several sports. Early specialization is actively discouraged.

Community

Sport as a place where a diverse city meets, competes and belongs together.

Integrity

Transparent fees, honest communication with families, accountable non-profit management.

Governance & Staff

Board of Directors & Coaching Staff

National Athletics Academy Inc. maintains a clear separation between volunteer non-profit community governance (Board of Directors) and certified day-to-day court operations.

Have questions regarding court schedules or daily sessions? Reach operations at (306) 330-9559 or info@naacanada.org.
