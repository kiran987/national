Parent Logistics & Gym Guidelines

First Practice? What to Bring & Where to Go

Youth sports should feel straightforward for busy parents. Here are the everyday logistics for drop-offs, shoes, and gym doors across Regina.

👟

What Your Child Needs to Bring

  • ✓Clean indoor court shoes: Required for hardwood floors. Outdoor runners worn outside are not permitted on the gym floor.
  • ✓Refillable water bottle: Pre-filled and labelled with your athlete's name.
  • ✓Gym clothes: T-shirt, athletic shorts or track pants. We provide all racquets, shuttles, balls, and pads.

Bring your own racket. Yonex shuttles are provided on court.

📍

Knox-Met Church (Fall Sessions)

  • 🚪Entrance door: Enter through the front doors of the church directly on Victoria Avenue (2340 Victoria Ave).
  • 🚗Parking tips: Free street parking on Victoria Ave, additional stalls in the back alley lot and on the right side of the building, plus Lorne Street.
  • ⏱️Arrival timing: Please arrive 5–10 minutes before session time to change into dry indoor court shoes in the hallway.

Coaches and check-in greeters are stationed right inside the main lobby doors.

👀

Check-In & Spectator Seating

  • 📋Coach check-in: Greet coach table inside the fellowship hall lobby for attendance and court assignments.
  • 🪑Parent seating: Chairs provided along the perimeter for parents who wish to stay and watch practices.
  • 📱TeamLinkt live reminders: Weather updates, court announcements, and roster notes are sent directly via TeamLinkt.

Have questions on the way? Call coach desk directly at (306) 330-9559.

Answers for Regina Families

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything parents need to know about locations, gear, coaches, and registrations.

Where do we enter and park at Knox-Met Church?↓

For Fall 2026 sessions at Knox-Met Church (2340 Victoria Ave), enter through the front doors of the church on Victoria Avenue. Free street parking is available on Victoria Ave, additional stalls in the back alley lot and on the right side of the building, plus street parking along Lorne Street. Directions, check-in tables, and room assignments are always posted live in your TeamLinkt schedule.

What should my child wear and bring to practice?↓

Athletes need clean, non-marking indoor court shoes (outdoor runners are strictly not permitted on the hardwood floor to protect the courts), comfortable gym clothes/shorts, and a labelled refillable water bottle. NAA supplies all sport equipment, including high-grade Yonex shuttles, Mikasa/Wilson training balls, and safety pads. Badminton players bring their own racket.

Can complete beginners join, or is prior experience required?↓

Beginners are warmly welcomed! Our foundational tiers (Smash Juniors, Spike Juniors, Junior Hoops, and National Shield Juniors) are specifically designed for introductory motor literacy and positive skill building. Coaches divide courts by age and ability so every child feels confident and supported.

How does the Family & Multi-Child Discount work?↓

We offer a 10% discount starting on your second registered participant (and each additional child). Complete registration for Child 1, then click "+ Register Another Participant" before paying. Your 10% multi-child discount is applied automatically by the TeamLinkt registration system at checkout when the registration is eligible.

Are financial assistance and installment plans available?↓

Yes. NAA offers flexible 2–3 installment payment options and a 10% multi-child sibling discount on second and subsequent children, applied automatically through the TeamLinkt system at checkout.

What coach screening and safe sport protocols are enforced?↓

100% of NAA coaches hold clear Vulnerable Sector Criminal Record Checks (CRC-VSS) issued by the Regina Police Service, have completed Sask Sport Respect in Sport training, and hold Standard First Aid / CPR-C. We strictly enforce the CAC Rule of Two: all training occurs in open, observable gymnasiums with two screened adults present.
