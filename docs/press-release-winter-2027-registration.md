# National Athletics Academy Opens Winter 2027 Registration for National Smash Badminton and National Spike Volleyball

*Five youth tiers, two evenings a week, ten sessions at the University of Regina Green Gym*

**REGINA, Sask. (November 2, 2026)** – National Athletics Academy Inc. (NAA) today announced that online registration is open for its Winter 2027 programs: **National Smash – Badminton** and **National Spike – Volleyball**. Both programs run on Tuesday and Thursday evenings at the University of Regina Green Gym, 3737 Wascana Parkway, from January 26 to March 11, 2027.

Each program is structured as ten coached sessions. There are no sessions from February 9 to 18.

## National Smash – Badminton

National Smash offers three tiers for young players:

| Tier | Ages | Schedule (Tue & Thu) | Tuition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Smash Juniors | 6–10 | Batch 1: 6:00–6:45 PM, or Batch 2: 6:45–7:30 PM | $150 |
| Smash Elite | 11–16 | Batch 1: 6:00–6:45 PM, or Batch 2: 6:45–7:30 PM | $175 |
| Smash Pro | 10–16 | 6:00–7:30 PM (90-minute sessions) | $295 |

Smash Juniors and Smash Elite are open, mixed-level classes where beginners and more experienced players train together. Smash Pro is an intensive batch focused on tactical training and matchplay.

## National Spike – Volleyball

National Spike offers two tiers:

| Tier | Ages | Schedule (Tue & Thu) | Tuition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Spike Juniors | 8–12 | 6:00–7:15 PM (75-minute sessions) | $195 |
| Spike Elite | 13–16 | 6:00–7:30 PM (90-minute sessions) | $245 |

## Registration and Fees

Families register online through TeamLinkt:

- National Smash – Badminton: https://app.teamlinkt.com/register/find/nationalathleticsacademy?cid=81569
- National Spike – Volleyball: https://app.teamlinkt.com/register/find/nationalathleticsacademy?cid=81602

Tuition can be paid in three installments. A mandatory Training Kit fee of $30 per athlete applies and is paid in full. Eligible sibling discounts are applied automatically through the TeamLinkt system at checkout. Athletes bring their own racket for badminton, and NAA provides the practice shuttles.

## Coming Up: National Smash Open 2027

NAA will also host the National Smash Open 2027, a one-day badminton tournament on eight courts at the University of Regina on February 27, 2027. Tournament registration opens soon.

## About National Athletics Academy

National Athletics Academy Inc. is a registered non-profit corporation in Saskatchewan delivering youth sports programs in Regina. Program details are available at https://naacanada.org.
