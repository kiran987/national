Why NAA?

Why Coach with National Athletics Academy?

NAA is building a coaching culture centred on safety, development, and community. Here's what you get when you join our team.

💰

Paid & Volunteer Positions

Both volunteer and paid coaching roles are available depending on program and experience.

📋

Structured Curriculum Provided

Full weekly lesson plans, drill libraries, and curriculum guides supplied — no need to start from scratch.

🎓

Certification Support

NAA supports coaches in obtaining sport certifications and Respect in Sport where not already held.

📈

Real Coaching Experience

Work with structured age cohorts, certified peers, and a growing community organization.

🤝

Community Impact

Shape youth sport development in Regina and build lasting relationships with families across the city.

⏰

Flexible Scheduling

Evening and weekend sessions across fall, winter, and spring blocks — designed around working adults.

Safe Sport First

Coaching Requirements

Every NAA coach must meet our Safe Sport standards before their first session. We support coaches in obtaining certifications where needed.

🔍

Criminal Record Check + VSS

All coaches must hold a valid CRC with Vulnerable Sector Screening before working with participants.

🏅

Respect in Sport (Activity Leader)

Required for all NAA coaches and activity leaders, covering safe sport, abuse prevention, and inclusion.

🩺

Standard First Aid + CPR-C

Current certification in Standard First Aid and CPR-C with AED is required.

🧠

Concussion Recognition

Parachute Canada Concussion Recognition Tool or equivalent must be on file.

🎓

NCCP Coaching Certification

NCCP sport certification (or willingness to complete one). NAA supports motivated coaches in obtaining sport-specific certifications where needed.
