Volleyball demands explosive agility, exceptional hand-eye coordination, and split-second teamwork. The NAA Volleyball Academy provides Regina youth with a technical foundation that standard school physical education cannot match. Led by screened, Safe Sport–trained coaches (including NCCP Volleyball Canada trained leadership), players master ball control mechanics in structured drills and small-sided modified games.
