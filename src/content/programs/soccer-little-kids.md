faqs:
  - question: Do parents need to be on the court during sessions?
    answer: For our U2 cohort (ages 24–36 months), one parent or guardian is required on the court to assist their toddler. For U3 and U4–U5 cohorts, parents can sit comfortably in the spectator area, though coaches welcome parent encouragement whenever a child needs extra reassurance.
  - question: What footwear and equipment do little kids need?
    answer: Athletes need comfortable clean indoor sneakers or non-marking court shoes and casual active clothing. Cleats are not required for indoor gym sessions. Shin guards are recommended for U4–U5 cohorts.
  - question: How is this program connected to Wascana FC?
    answer: This program is officially delivered in direct association with Wascana FC. The curriculum combines Wascana FC's soccer methodology with NAA's multi-sport physical literacy framework, offering a seamless pathway into competitive youth soccer as children grow.
  - question: What happens if my child is shy or hesitant on day one?
    answer: Our coaches are trained specifically in early childhood engagement. We understand that toddlers take a session or two to acclimate to new environments. Our games are 100% positive, pressure-free, and designed to help every child feel safe, welcome, and excited at their own pace.

National Athletics Academy has partnered with Wascana FC — Regina's established community soccer club — to deliver a premier early childhood soccer curriculum specifically engineered for toddlers and young children aged 2 to 5 (U2–U5). Our play-based approach weaves imaginative story adventures with genuine physical literacy, teaching young children how to control their bodies, run with balance, change direction, and kick with joy.
