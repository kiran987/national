    answer: 'Every National Shield coach holds a Criminal Record Check plus Vulnerable Sector Check, Respect in Sport (Activity Leader), NCCP Safe Sport Training, Standard First Aid + CPR-C (AED), and concussion recognition certification. NAA follows the Rule of Two: interactions between coaches and participants always take place in open, observable settings with two screened adults present, never one-on-one behind closed doors.'
  - question: What is the SHIELD Safety System?
    answer: The SHIELD Safety System is National Shield's intellectual signature — a structured personal safety framework taught every cycle. S = See (awareness of surroundings), H = Have boundaries, I = Intentionally use your voice, E = Exit, L = Locate help, D = Describe & report. Physical escape technique is taught only after the first five principles are demonstrably understood.
  - question: What is the 12-Week Foundation Cycle?
    answer: Each National Shield block runs as a defined 12-week cycle — Weeks 1–4 BUILD, Weeks 5–8 PROTECT, Weeks 9–12 PERFORM — ending with a Challenge Day where parents attend a demonstration and children receive achievement recognition. This creates urgency, a natural renewal point, and a far more meaningful experience than open-ended drop-in classes.
  - question: What does my child need to bring?
    answer: Comfortable athletic clothing and clean indoor footwear. All training pads, mitts, and equipment are provided. Beginners do not need to purchase any martial arts gear to start.

National Shield - Martial Arts is a fitness-focused, safety-first personal development program operated by National Athletics Academy Inc. Through age-appropriate martial arts training, structured fitness challenges, the SHIELD Safety System, and the BRAVE Anti-Bullying Program, participants build physical strength, mental confidence, and real personal safety awareness.

**We do not teach children to look for fights. We teach them to avoid them, respond confidently, stay safe, and get help.**

The program runs on a 12-Week Foundation Cycle across three age streams — Juniors (5–12), Youth (13–17), and Pro (18+ & Super-Seniors) — with a 5-level achievement system, and monthly Shield Challenge Days.
