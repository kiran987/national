faqs:
  - question: Do athletes need track spikes?
    answer: Spikes are not required for our junior and intermediate cohorts; standard clean running shoes with good cushion and traction are ideal. Senior athletes training on rubberized track surfaces may use standard 5mm pyramid indoor spikes if desired.
  - question: How does Athletics help my child in other sports like hockey, basketball, or soccer?
    answer: Athletics teaches the core biomechanics of acceleration, top-end sprint speed, change of direction, and explosive jumping. Athletes who train in track consistently demonstrate superior speed and endurance in their primary team sports.

    answer: Sessions take place at indoor fieldhouses, multi-sport gymnasiums, and outdoor track facilities during spring and summer blocks.

Athletics (Track & Field) is the cornerstone of every major sport. Whether an athlete aims to compete in track meets or gain explosive speed for basketball, soccer, volleyball, or hockey, the NAA Athletics Academy develops proper sprint mechanics, stride length/frequency balance, and elastic power under certified track coaches.
