**Bring your smash. Claim your glory.** On **Saturday, February 27, 2027**, National Athletics Academy is bringing the most electric single-day badminton championship of the year to the **KHS Gymnasium at the University of Regina**.

Built for both rising junior prodigies (`U11` through `U19`) and competitive adult players (`MS`, `WS`, `MD`, `WD`, `XD`), **[National Smash Open 2027](/programs/tournaments/badminton/national-smash-open-2027/)** is a streamlined, high-energy one-day showdown across **8 dedicated indoor courts** and **20 championship brackets**.

Online registration officially opens on **November 1, 2026**, and strict player caps apply to every division on a first-come, first-served basis.

## Why We Built National Smash Open 2027

Anyone who has spent a weekend at a traditional badminton tournament knows the biggest frustration for athletes and parents alike: playing a 20-minute match, then sitting in the bleachers for four hours waiting for your next court call.

We designed **National Smash Open 2027** from the ground up to fix that. By enforcing strict entry caps per bracket and running **8 indoor courts simultaneously** inside the University of Regina's KHS Gymnasium, every competitor gets intense, well-timed court action without hours of wasted downtime.

Every registered athlete receives:

- **8 Dedicated Indoor Courts** running simultaneously from first serve to championship finals
- **Fast-Paced Championship Schedule** engineered for a punctual 6:00 PM finish
- **Premium Tournament Shuttles** supplied for all matches
- **Custom Medals & Championship Trophies** across all 20 divisions
- **Grand Unified Awards Podium Ceremony** from 5:00 PM – 6:00 PM

Because National Smash Open 2027 is an **independent open tournament**, no Badminton Canada or Sask Badminton membership is required to enter. Whether you train with our [National Smash Badminton Academy](/programs/badminton/), play on your school team, compete at a local club, or play recreationally across Saskatchewan, you are welcome on our courts.

## All 20 Tournament Categories & Entry Rules

Both Singles and Doubles divisions are offered across every age tier. To keep court rotations moving on schedule, athletes may enter a **maximum of 2 events**:

- **Youth Athletes:** `1 Singles` + `1 Open Doubles`
- **Adult Competitors:** `1 Singles` + `1 Gender Doubles (MD/WD)` **OR** `1 Mixed Doubles (XD)` *(cannot enter both MD/WD and XD)*

### Junior Divisions (15 Brackets — Age as of December 31, 2027)

- **Under-11 (U11):** Boys Singles | Girls Singles | Open Doubles
- **Under-13 (U13):** Boys Singles | Girls Singles | Open Doubles
- **Under-15 (U15):** Boys Singles | Girls Singles | Open Doubles
- **Under-17 (U17):** Boys Singles | Girls Singles | Open Doubles
- **Under-19 (U19):** Boys Singles | Girls Singles | Open Doubles

### Adult Open Championship Divisions (5 Brackets)

- **Men's Singles (MS)**
- **Women's Singles (WS)**
- **Men's Doubles (MD)**
- **Women's Doubles (WD)**
- **Mixed Doubles (XD)**

## Part of NAA's Continuous Annual Badminton Circuit (Winter & Summer)

National Smash Open 2027 kicks off a permanent, yearly tradition in Regina. Each year, National Athletics Academy hosts **two flagship open badminton tournaments** at the University of Regina's KHS Gymnasium:

1. **Winter Championship (1-Day Showdown):** **Saturday, February 27, 2027** (`8:00 AM – 6:00 PM`) — Registration opens **November 1, 2026**.
2. **Summer Grand Championship (2-Day Prestigious Open):** **Saturday, June 19, 2027** (`8:00 AM – 7:30 PM`) & **Sunday, June 20, 2027** (`8:00 AM – 6:00 PM`) — Registration opens **March 1, 2027**.
